Data Protection Policy

Internal policy governing the collection, use, and protection of personal information by Templelite

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Privacy Policy · Terms and Conditions · Data Protection Policy

This Data Protection Policy ("Policy") sets out how Templelite Technologies ("Templelite", "the Company") collects, processes, stores, shares, and protects personal information, in compliance with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] (Act No. 5 of 2021) ("the CDPA") and its supporting regulations, including the Cyber and Data Protection (Licensing of Data Controllers and Appointment of Data Protection Officers) Regulations, 2024.

This Policy applies internally to all directors, employees, contractors, and third parties who process personal information on behalf of Templelite. It complements, and should be read alongside, the Company's external-facing Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

1. Purpose and Scope

This Policy exists to:

  • Ensure that personal information processed by the Company is handled lawfully, fairly, and transparently
  • Set out the responsibilities of staff and management in relation to data protection
  • Establish procedures for exercising data subject rights, managing data breaches, and engaging third-party processors
  • Support the Company's compliance with the CDPA and reduce the risk of regulatory, financial, and reputational harm

This Policy applies to all personal information processed by Templelite in any format — electronic or physical — including customer data, employee data, and data obtained through the Company's website, mobile application(s), and messaging channels such as the WhatsApp Business Platform.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Information" means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined in the CDPA.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on personal information, including collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, or destruction.
  • "Data Subject" means the individual to whom personal information relates.
  • "Data Controller" means Templelite, as the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal information.
  • "Data Processor" means any person or entity that processes personal information on behalf of Templelite, such as a hosting or software provider.
  • "Data Protection Officer" or "DPO" means the individual appointed by Templelite to oversee compliance with this Policy and the CDPA.
  • "Data Breach" means any incident resulting in the unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, alteration, or destruction of personal information.
  • "POTRAZ" means the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe, designated as the Data Protection Authority under the CDPA.

3. Data Protection Principles

In processing personal information, Templelite and all staff must ensure that personal information is:

  • Processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently
  • Collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes, and not further processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes
  • Adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed (data minimisation)
  • Accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date
  • Retained for no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed
  • Processed in a manner that ensures appropriate security, including protection against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction, or damage

4. Roles and Responsibilities

4.1 The Board / Senior Management

Senior management is ultimately accountable for data protection compliance across the Company, including ensuring adequate resourcing, approving this Policy, and ensuring Templelite holds a valid data controller licence with POTRAZ where required.

4.2 Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Templelite has appointed a Data Protection Officer, in accordance with the CDPA and its 2024 regulations, currently: Tawanda Zuze, zuzetawanda105@gmail.com. The DPO is responsible for:

  • Monitoring compliance with this Policy, the CDPA, and related regulations
  • Advising the Company on data protection obligations and best practice
  • Maintaining the Company's Register of Processing Activities
  • Acting as the primary point of contact for data subjects, POTRAZ, and other regulators
  • Coordinating the Company's response to data breaches and data subject requests
  • Supporting staff training and awareness on data protection

4.3 Employees and Contractors

All staff and contractors who handle personal information must:

  • Process personal information only for legitimate business purposes and in line with this Policy
  • Keep personal information secure and confidential, and never disclose it to unauthorised persons
  • Report any suspected data breach to the DPO immediately upon discovery
  • Complete data protection training as required by the Company

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Templelite will only process personal information where at least one lawful basis applies, namely:

  • Consent has been obtained from the data subject
  • Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with the data subject
  • Processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation
  • Processing is necessary for the Company's legitimate interests, provided these do not override the rights of the data subject

Where consent is relied upon, it must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, and must be capable of being withdrawn at any time.

6. Categories of Personal Information Processed

Templelite currently processes the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact information (name, phone number, email address)
  • Account information (username, business/organisation details, where applicable)
  • Communications data, including messages exchanged via the WhatsApp Business Platform
  • Basic technical/usage data collected via the Company's website and mobile application(s)

Templelite does not, as a matter of policy, collect special categories of personal information (such as biometric, health, or financial data) except where strictly necessary, disclosed in advance, and supported by explicit consent or another lawful basis recognised under the CDPA.

7. Data Subject Rights

Data subjects have the right to:

  • Be informed about how their personal information is processed
  • Access personal information held about them
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of their personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Object to, or request restriction of, certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with POTRAZ

All requests from data subjects must be forwarded to the DPO immediately. The DPO will verify the identity of the requester and respond within the timeframes prescribed by the CDPA. Staff must not respond to such requests directly without DPO involvement.

8. Data Retention and Disposal

Personal information must not be retained for longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. The DPO is responsible for maintaining a retention schedule for each category of personal information processed by the Company. When personal information is no longer required, it must be securely deleted, destroyed, or anonymised, using methods appropriate to the format in which it is held.

9. Data Security

Templelite will implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • Access controls, ensuring personal information is only accessible to staff who need it to perform their role
  • Use of strong, unique passwords and, where available, multi-factor authentication for systems holding personal information
  • Secure storage of physical records containing personal information
  • Regular review of access permissions and removal of access when no longer required (e.g., on termination of employment)
  • Due diligence and contractual safeguards when engaging third-party data processors

10. Engaging Third-Party Processors

Where Templelite engages a third party to process personal information on its behalf (for example, a hosting provider, or Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for messaging), the Company will:

  • Conduct reasonable due diligence on the third party's data protection practices before engagement
  • Ensure a written agreement is in place setting out the processor's obligations, where practicable
  • Limit the personal information shared with the third party to what is strictly necessary for the service provided

Templelite does not share personal information with third parties for their own independent purposes, and does not sell personal information.

11. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Where personal information is transferred outside Zimbabwe — for example, because a service provider's infrastructure is hosted abroad, or because messaging data is processed by Meta outside Zimbabwe — the DPO must be satisfied that appropriate safeguards are in place to ensure the information receives a level of protection consistent with the CDPA.

12. Data Breach Management

Any employee or contractor who becomes aware of, or suspects, a data breach must report it to the DPO immediately, and in any event without delay. On becoming aware of a data breach, the DPO will:

  • Assess the nature, scope, and likely impact of the breach
  • Take immediate steps to contain the breach and mitigate its effects
  • Notify POTRAZ within the timeframe prescribed by the CDPA (currently 24 hours from becoming aware of a breach)
  • Notify affected data subjects without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a risk to their rights and freedoms
  • Document the breach, its causes, and the remedial action taken, for the Company's internal records

13. Records of Processing Activities

The DPO will maintain a Register of Processing Activities, recording the categories of personal information processed, the purposes of processing, the lawful basis relied upon, data recipients, retention periods, and security measures applied. This Register will be reviewed and updated at least annually, or whenever processing activities materially change.

14. Data Protection Impact Assessments

Before introducing a new system, product, or process that involves significant processing of personal information, or that is likely to result in a high risk to data subjects, the relevant team must consult the DPO to determine whether a Data Protection Impact Assessment is required, and, if so, complete one before processing begins.

15. Training and Awareness

All employees and contractors who handle personal information will receive data protection training upon induction and at regular intervals thereafter. The DPO is responsible for coordinating this training and maintaining training records.

16. Monitoring, Audit, and Enforcement

The DPO will periodically review the Company's compliance with this Policy and the CDPA, including through internal audits where appropriate. Failure to comply with this Policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and may expose the Company and responsible individuals to regulatory penalties under the CDPA.

17. Policy Review

This Policy will be reviewed at least annually, or sooner where required by changes in Applicable Law, regulatory guidance, or the Company's processing activities. The "Effective date" at the top of this Policy reflects the date it was last approved.
