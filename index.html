About Templelite

A Zimbabwean technology partner, built on competence and supply.

Founded in 2017 as a provider of systems and software solutions, Templelite is a wholly incorporated Zimbabwean company based in Harare. Serving clients in every part of the country, we have grown into a premium provider of converged business solutions, custom software engineering, and large-scale distribution of ICT products and consumables.

We take the time to understand your needs, then meet them with high-quality, high-value products. Strategic channel distribution and partnerships with major global brands keep our clients in step with new technology, and their cyber ecosystems secure.
