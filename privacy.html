2. Definitions

  • "Personal Information" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under the CDPA.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on personal information, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or destruction.
  • "Data Subject" means the individual to whom personal information relates.
  • "Data Controller" means the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal information — in this case, Templelite.
  • "Meta" refers to Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates, including WhatsApp Ireland Limited, which provide the WhatsApp Business Platform used by Templelite.

3. Personal Information We Collect

We collect only the categories of personal information reasonably necessary to provide our services. This currently consists of basic contact and account information, namely:

  • Full name
  • Phone number, including your WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Account credentials and account-related information (e.g., username, business/organisation name where applicable)
  • The content of messages you send us and metadata associated with those messages (e.g., timestamps, delivery status) when you contact us via WhatsApp or other messaging channels
  • Basic technical information generated automatically when you use our website or app, such as IP address, browser/device type, and pages visited

We do not currently collect financial, biometric, health, or government identification data through the services covered by this Policy. Should this change, we will update this Policy and, where required by the CDPA, obtain your prior consent.

4. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you, when you register an account, fill in a form on our website or app, or message us
  • Automatically, through standard website/app functionality (e.g., server logs)
  • Through the WhatsApp Business Platform (operated by Meta), when you initiate or respond to a conversation with us on WhatsApp

5. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

In accordance with the CDPA, we process your personal information only where we have a lawful basis to do so, including:

  • Your consent, given freely when you opt in to contact us or register an account
  • Performance of a contract, to provide the services you request
  • Compliance with a legal obligation, including obligations under the CDPA and applicable regulatory requirements
  • Our legitimate interests, such as maintaining the security of our systems and improving our services, provided these do not override your rights and interests

We use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support, including via WhatsApp
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website, app, and services
  • Communicate with you about our services, including service-related notices
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations

6. Use of the WhatsApp Business Platform and Other Meta Services

Templelite uses the WhatsApp Business API, provided by Meta, to communicate with customers. When you message us on WhatsApp, your message content and associated metadata are transmitted through Meta's infrastructure and are subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, in addition to this Policy.

In connection with our use of the WhatsApp Business Platform:

  • We only send you messages you have consented to receive, or that are necessary to respond to a conversation you have initiated, in line with Meta's Business Messaging Policy.
  • We do not use information obtained via WhatsApp for purposes unrelated to providing our services, and we do not sell such information.
  • Meta acts as our data processor / platform provider for the limited purpose of delivering messages between you and us; Meta's own handling of your information is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy, available at https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy and https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/.
  • We do not integrate other Meta products (such as Facebook Login, Meta Pixel, or the Facebook/Instagram Graph API) into our services beyond the WhatsApp Business Platform, unless expressly stated to you at the point of collection.

7. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We disclose personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • To Meta, solely to the extent necessary to operate the WhatsApp Business Platform, as described in Section 6
  • To service providers who process data strictly on our behalf and under our instructions (e.g., IT hosting or infrastructure providers), and who are contractually bound to protect your information
  • Where required by law, regulation, court order, or a lawful request from POTRAZ or another competent authority
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent protections being maintained

Aside from the above, Templelite does not share your personal information with any other third party.

8. Cross-Border Transfer of Data

Because the WhatsApp Business Platform is operated by Meta, messages you send us via WhatsApp may be processed and stored outside Zimbabwe. Where we transfer personal information outside Zimbabwe, we take reasonable steps, consistent with the CDPA, to ensure the recipient provides an adequate level of protection for your information, including through contractual safeguards where applicable.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When personal information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or destruction. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify the Data Protection Authority (POTRAZ) and, where required, affected data subjects, in accordance with the timelines prescribed by the CDPA.

11. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the CDPA, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is processed
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal retention requirements
  • Object to, or request restriction of, certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint with POTRAZ, as Zimbabwe's designated Data Protection Authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@templelite.co.zw or templelite@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within the timeframes required by the CDPA.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a parent or guardian as required by the CDPA. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.
